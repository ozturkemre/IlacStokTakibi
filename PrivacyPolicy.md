# Privacy Policy for İlac Stok Takibi

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how İlac Stok Takibi ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information. 

### 1. Information Collection and Storage
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, account information, or location data on external servers.
- **Local Data Storage:** All medicine records, quantities, and expiration dates entered or scanned by the user are stored strictly locally on your device using a local SQLite/Room database. If you uninstall the application, this data is permanently deleted.

### 2. Device Permissions Used
- **Camera (android.permission.CAMERA):** Used exclusively for real-time scanning of GS1 DataMatrix and barcodes on medicine packaging via Google ML Kit. The app does NOT capture, save, record, or transmit photos, videos, or image feeds to any third party or remote server. Processing happens entirely on-device.
- **Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS):** Used solely to schedule and deliver local alerts on your device when a medicine's expiration date is approaching.
- **Boot Completed (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED):** Used to reschedule local expiration date reminder checks automatically when the device restarts.

### 3. Third-Party Services
The application utilizes Google ML Kit for on-device barcode recognition. No data or scanned images are sold, rented, or distributed to advertisers or data brokers.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
The app does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page.
